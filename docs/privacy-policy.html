Privacy Policy

Kimeo Games · Effective 18 November 2026

This policy applies to the mobile games published on Google Play by Kimeo Games ("we", "us"). It explains what information the games use and how it is handled.

1. What we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Game progress, coins, settings and similar data are stored only on your device. We do not receive them and cannot see them. Uninstalling a game removes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games show ads using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use, from your device:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID;
  • approximate location (derived from your IP address);
  • how you interact with the app and with ads;
  • crash logs and diagnostic and performance data.

This data is used for showing ads (including personalised ads where permitted), analytics, and fraud prevention and security. Google acts as an independent provider of this service. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the games show a consent form on first launch. You can accept or decline personalised ads there.
  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You choose when to watch one.

4. Purchases

If a game offers an in-app purchase (such as removing ads), payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

5. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. The only third party that receives data through the games is Google, for the advertising purposes above. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete; data held by Google is governed by Google's policies and your Google account controls.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at github.com/karamahmed-dev/Kiemo-games/issues.
